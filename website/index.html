How it works

  1. Install the extension Add Trackmail to Chrome and sign in with Google.
  2. Enable tracking in compose Flip "Track email" on before you hit Send.
  3. Send through Gmail Your email goes out normally — with an invisible tracking pixel.
  4. See when it's opened Check status in the extension popup or your tracked list.

Questions

How does email tracking work?

A tiny invisible image (tracking pixel) is embedded in your email. When the recipient's email client loads images, our server records an open event.

Do I need a separate email app?

No. Trackmail enhances Gmail's existing compose window. You never leave Gmail.

How accurate is open tracking?

An open means the pixel was fetched — often via Gmail's image proxy — not proof a human read the email. Some opens may be false positives from prefetching.

Is it free?

Yes for the MVP. The stack runs locally for $0 during development — PostgreSQL, Go backend, Chrome extension.

What data do you collect?

Recipient address, subject line, open timestamps, and aggregated open counts. We do not store email body content.
